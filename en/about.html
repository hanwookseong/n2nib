ABOUT US · INSURANCE BROKER

An insurance broker that works for you.

Corporate insurance has long wordings and complex conditions. For the same risk, the cover and the premium can differ greatly depending on which insurer and which wording or endorsement you choose. A broker is an independent advisor who compares the market on your behalf, negotiates wordings, and stands with you through the claim when a loss occurs.

N2N Insurance Brokerage is an FSS-registered insurance broker under Article 89 of the Insurance Business Act. Our representative, Hanwook Seong (ACIU underwriter), personally advises on risk analysis, multi-insurer quoting, wording negotiation and claims.

01 / DEFINITION

What is an insurance broker?

An insurance broker is a party that independently intermediates the conclusion of insurance contracts and is registered with the Financial Supervisory Service (FSS) under Article 89 of the Insurance Business Act.

In the primary market a broker acts for the policyholder (the client); in the reinsurance market it may act for the cedant or the reinsurer. To become a broker one must pass the FSS examination and register with the Financial Services Commission.

In other words, a broker does not represent any single insurer. Instead, on the client's side, it compares the wordings, rates, financial strength and claims-handling of several insurers to find the most favourable terms, and supports the indemnity claim on the client's behalf when a loss occurs.

— Insurance Business Act Art. 2(11) · Art. 89 · Art. 93 (FSS insurance-broker registration)

02 / DIFFERENCE

Buying direct vs. through a broker,
what's the difference?

An insurer's sales force represents the insurer. A broker represents the client. That simple difference drives the cover, the premium and even the claim outcome. Here are 5 limits companies often hit when dealing with insurers directly in Korea — and how a broker resolves them.

Aspect Buying direct from an insurer Through an insurance broker
Agency relationship That insurer represents its own position The broker represents the client's position
Range of proposals One product from that one insurer Simultaneous quotes from up to 6 insurers, compared
Wording negotiation Standard wording & exclusions applied as-is Negotiates added endorsements, reduced exclusions and limits on your behalf
Claims You handle the insurer's process yourself The broker assists with the claim and any appeal
At renewal The insurer prompts an automatic renewal Provides an annual market re-assessment and alternative quotes

※ Under Article 89 of the Insurance Business Act a broker operates independently of insurers and works for the policyholder (the client), not the insurer.

C · WORDING NEGOTIATION

Negotiate wording & endorsements

Beyond the standard wording, we negotiate added endorsements, reduced exclusions, retroactive dates and lower deductibles. For high-risk sectors where standard underwriting is declined, we secure capacity via the reinsurance market. This is possible only with a broker's market standing.

D · CLAIMS ADVOCACY

Stand with you on claims

When a loss occurs we handle claim documents, loss-adjustment and appeals on your side. Because an ACIU underwriter familiar with insurer wordings negotiates directly, we prevent the missed or delayed indemnity that often arises when individuals claim on their own.

E · RENEWAL REVIEW

Review the market at renewal

At each renewal we re-assess market movement, rate trends and new products. Rather than simply following the current insurer's auto-renewal, we present alternative quotes where the market offers better terms, helping with premium savings and coverage improvements.
