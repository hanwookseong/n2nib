GUIDE · DATA CENTRE INSURANCE SERIES

Data Centre SLA Exposure
Contract first, then the cover tracks

An SLA is a contract promise, not an insurance product. Breaching it can mean service credits, damages claims and lost customers. This page sets out the exposures and which insurance tracks respond — and which do not.

Written by Hanwook Seong — ACIU · Licensed insurance broker, FSS Reg. No. 2026-012201
Last updated 2026-11-08 · General explanation based on wording structures and insurers' materials; actual cover depends on product, wording and underwriting. · 한국어

KEY POINTS

  • Service credits are often designed as fee reductions, which standard liability wordings commonly do not cover.
  • Without a sole-and-exclusive-remedy clause, customers may claim actual damages on top of credits — often a larger exposure.
  • Negligent service failures point to tech E&O; hacking to cyber; physical damage to property and BI.
  • Downtime without physical damage (grid failure, software) usually falls outside property and BI.
  • Index-based (parametric) downtime covers exist overseas; their application in Korea is still being examined. N2N does not currently offer placement of this type of cover.

03

Basic cover and main exclusions

What it covers

  • Tech E&O: customers' damages claims arising from negligent service failures
  • Cyber: liability and response costs from attacks, and cyber business interruption
  • Property + BI: own revenue loss following insured physical damage

Main exclusions

  • Service credits designed as fee reductions (commonly)
  • Performance guarantees
  • Downtime without physical damage under property/BI
  • Contractual liability beyond what the law would impose (depending on wording)

Policyholder and insureds

  • Align the SLA outage formula, exclusions and caps with the insurance tracks before negotiating cover.

Scenarios

Cooling outage after switchboard failure

A switchboard failure stops the chillers; backup does not include cooling and some halls exceed temperature limits.

Likely position — Credits and a customer damages claim are two different exposures. The damages claim may be considered under professional liability if negligence is established; plant damage under property; the credits are excluded under many wordings. (standard textbook scenario)

Frequently asked questions

Is there such a thing as SLA insurance?

Not as a single standard product. SLA exposure is usually addressed by combining contract terms with tech E&O, cyber and property/BI cover.

Are service credits insured?

Credits designed as fee reductions are commonly excluded from liability wordings. Check the contract and the wording together.

Can N2N place parametric downtime cover?

Not at present. We describe the structure for information only.
