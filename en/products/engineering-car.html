Overview

Contractors All Risks (CAR) is an all-risks policy covering, under a single contract, both the physical loss to the works and third-party liability that can arise during civil and building construction.

With the principal, main contractor and subcontractors as co-insureds, it provides unified indemnity even where responsibility is unclear. CAR is often a mandatory bid / contract condition for public and large private works.

Key features

  • 01
    Property + liability combined

    Section I (material damage) and Section II (third-party liability) in one cover.

  • 02
    Co-insured structure

    Principal, contractor, partners and designer can all be co-insured.

  • 03
    Testing & maintenance extension

    Cover can extend to the commissioning period and the Defects Liability Period (DLP).

  • 04
    Tailored by work type

    Underwriting reflects the risk of each work type — civil, building, remodeling, underground.

Who needs it

  • 01
    Building / civil contractors

    General and specialty contractors, SOC contractors.

  • 02
    Public / private principals

    Government works, LH, public corporations and private developers.

  • 03
    EPC project operators

    Plant, renewable-energy and infrastructure EPC consortia.

Main losses covered

  • Section I (material damage): loss to the works, temporary works and construction plant from fire, explosion, lightning, wind/flood
  • Material damage arising from design / workmanship / material defects (varies by DE / LEG clause)
  • Theft / damage of construction materials on site
  • Section II (liability): legal liability for third-party injury / property damage during the works
  • Debris removal, emergency prevention, drawing-reinstatement costs (endorsement)

Main endorsements

  • Commissioning (Testing) period extension
  • Defects Liability Period (DLP)
  • Damage to neighbouring buildings from vibration / demolition / subsidence
  • Design-defect cover (LEG 2 / LEG 3)
  • Cross Liability

Main exclusions

  • Cost of redoing defective workmanship itself (LEG 1 basis)
  • Indirect loss from delay or under-performance
  • Contractual penalties / liquidated damages
  • War, civil commotion, strike, nuclear risk
  • Works already handed over or in use (without notice / endorsement)

What we need to quote

  • Construction contract (contract value, period)
  • Project outline, work-type breakdown, location, ground-survey report
  • List of contractor / subcontractors (for co-insured registration)
  • Sum insured for temporary works and plant
  • Construction-accident history for the last 5 years

Other notes

  • Notify immediately and pay additional premium when the contract value increases or the period is extended.
  • Notify separately for a long suspension (3 months or more).
  • Cover for design defects differs by LEG level (LEG 1 / 2 / 3).

Cooling-off / duty of disclosure

  • Cooling-off (cancellation) within 15 days of receiving the certificate (excluding professional financial consumers).
  • Breach of the duty to explain material matters or a missing signature may allow cancellation within 3 months.
  • Breach of the duty of disclosure may lead to cancellation or reduced indemnity.
