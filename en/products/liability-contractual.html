Overview

Contractual liability covers monetary loss arising from a heightened indemnity clause or an SLA-breach obligation set out in a B2B contract.

It supplements the “liability assumed under contract” exclusion in standard CGL / PL and includes assumed-performance indemnity.

Key features

  • 01
    Indemnity-clause cover

    Answers heightened indemnity obligations written into contracts.

  • 02
    Supplements CGL / PL

    Fills the contractual-liability exclusion in standard liability policies.

  • 03
    SLA-breach exposure

    Addresses service-level and delivery-breach indemnity.

  • 04
    Hold-harmless support

    Supports performance of hold-harmless undertakings.

Who needs it

  • 01
    EPC contractors

    Projects carrying heavy indemnity clauses.

  • 02
    IT outsourcing

    SLA-driven service providers.

  • 03
    Service contractors

    Vendors assuming contractual indemnity.

  • 04
    B2B suppliers

    Suppliers signing hold-harmless terms.

Main losses covered

  • Indemnity payable under a contractual indemnity clause
  • Compensation for SLA / delivery breaches
  • Performance of hold-harmless clauses
  • Liquidated damages for non-performance (in part)
  • Defense costs

Main exclusions

  • Invalidity or cancellation of the contract itself
  • Willful acts and fraud
  • Debt-type undertakings (monetary obligations)
  • Fines and taxes
  • Loss arising from unlawful acts

Conditions & process

  • Auto-triggered liquidated damages (with no proof of loss) typically fall within policy exclusions — confirm in advance whether such clauses are covered
  • Cover supplements, and does not replace, CGL / PL
  • Final terms, limits and premium are confirmed after insurer underwriting

  • 03
    SLAs

Other notes

  • Final terms, limits and premium are confirmed after insurer underwriting
