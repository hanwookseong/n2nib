When you need driving academy package insurance

Common situations in driving-academy operation — a five-scenario self-check

🚗

Driving academies where students drive vehicles themselves during instruction

Road and skills instruction involves inexperienced students driving directly, so there is a constant risk of instruction-vehicle collisions and contact accidents.

🧑‍🏫

When an instructor or examiner rides along to supervise

An accident while an instructor or examiner rides along for instruction or assessment can lead to the academy's liability.

🏫

Academies operating buildings and skills courses

Fire, theft and defect incidents at academy facilities — teaching halls, skills courses, parking — lead to facility damage and third-party liability.

🏍️

When operating a motorcycle teaching room

Motorcycle and scooter instruction differs in accident pattern from car instruction, so a motorcycle extension should be considered separately.

🌙

Academies instructing at night or in adverse weather

Instruction at night or in rain has higher accident risk, so a design suited to the operating hours — a night-instruction endorsement — is needed.

A dispute pattern seen in the field

A driving academy easily sees “an accident a student causes while driving” as the student's responsibility, but accidents during instruction are bound up with the academy's management and supervision responsibility. Instruction-vehicle collisions, accidents while an instructor or examiner rides along, and accidents from defects in the skills course or fixtures can all lead to the academy's liability. Driving academy package insurance bundles student injury during instruction, instructor and examiner on-duty accidents, instruction-vehicle damage, academy facility damage and third-party liability into one design. However, you should also understand that accidents while breaching statutory requirements — operating with unqualified instructors, an unlicensed student road driving — fall within the exclusions.

Three things easily missed when buying driving academy package insurance

The wording and structure points decision-makers most often overlook

  • 1

    Unqualified operation and unlicensed road driving are excluded

    Accidents while operating with unqualified instructors or during an unlicensed student's road driving are excluded. Separately from holding cover, the operating requirements of the Road Traffic Act must be met.

  • 2

    Instruction-vehicle cover and third-party liability are separate areas

    Damage to the instruction vehicle itself (own vehicle) and liability for loss caused to others are separately covered. Check which modules to include for your vehicles and operation.

  • 3

    Road driving, motorcycles and night instruction need separate endorsements

    Road-driving-only, motorcycle instruction and night/adverse-weather instruction can leave gaps under base cover alone. Check that the endorsements matching your actual instruction are included.

Frequently asked questions

The questions asked most when considering driving academy package insurance

How does it differ from general academy liability insurance?

Where general academy liability centres on user compensation during facilities and instruction, driving academy package insurance — suited to students driving vehicles themselves — bundles instruction-vehicle damage, road-driving accidents and ride-along instructor/examiner accidents into one design.

Is an accident a student causes during road driving covered too?

Road-driving accidents during lawful instruction can be designed into cover. However, accidents breaching statutory requirements — an unlicensed student road driving — are excluded, and you should check whether the road-driving-only endorsement is included.

Is damage to the instruction vehicle itself covered too?

Own-vehicle and own-body damage to the instruction vehicle can be designed as a module of the package. Check inclusion against the details of the instruction vehicles held.

Can a motorcycle teaching room be covered?

Motorcycle and scooter instruction, differing in accident pattern from car instruction, is handled by a motorcycle extension. Check that the extension matching the vehicle types taught is included.

When is cover limited?

Operating with unqualified instructors, road driving by an unlicensed student, instruction under alcohol or drugs, and clear breach of the academy's safety-management duty are excluded. Lawful operation under the Road Traffic Act is the premise of cover.

How is the premium calculated?

The insurer calculates it based on academy size, number of instruction vehicles, number of students, instruction type (road, motorcycle, night), the module structure and past incident history. The exact premium and terms are confirmed after underwriting by insurers such as AIG, Chubb, DB, Hyundai, KB and Meritz.
