Overview

Drone liability is a compulsory liability cover for third-party bodily injury and property damage caused by a drone (UAV) crashing, colliding or losing control during operation.

Corporate and individual use are underwritten on the same wording, with rates varying by purpose of use, number of units and weight.

Key features

  • 01
    Compulsory for commercial use

    Commercial drones and units over 25 kg must be insured under the Aviation Safety Act.

  • 02
    Crash & collision cover

    Third-party injury and property damage from crashes and collisions.

  • 03
    One wording, all uses

    Same wording for corporate and individual operators.

  • 04
    Rated by use & weight

    Rates vary by purpose, fleet size and unit weight.

Who needs it

  • 01
    Industrial operators

    Spraying, inspection and survey operators.

  • 02
    Filming & media

    Aerial-filming and photography operators.

  • 03
    Logistics & delivery

    Drone-delivery and logistics operators.

  • 04
    Any UAV operator

    Any business or individual flying a registered drone.

Main losses covered

  • Third-party bodily injury from a drone crash or collision
  • Property damage to ground facilities, vehicles and the like
  • Damage to nearby property from loss of control
  • Defense and litigation costs
  • First-aid and emergency-response costs

Main exclusions

  • Willful acts, gross negligence and unauthorised flight
  • Entry into no-fly zones
  • Crashes caused by inadequate maintenance
  • War, terrorism and nuclear risks
  • Physical damage to the drone itself (separate drone property cover)

Conditions & process

  • Keep the airframe registered/reported under the Aviation Safety Act and Aviation Business Act — incidents while unregistered may be excluded
  • Rates vary by purpose of use, fleet size and weight
  • Final terms, limits and premium are confirmed after insurer underwriting

What we need to quote

  • 01

    Business registration and corporate register.

  • 02
    Airframe details

    Drone registration certificate and airframe number.

  • 03
    Pilot & operations

    Pilot licence, flight history, operating area and times.

  • 04
    Claims history

    Past incidents.

Other notes

  • Registration and reporting of the airframe must be maintained separately from insurance
  • Final terms, limits and premium are confirmed after insurer underwriting
