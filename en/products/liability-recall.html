Overview

When a product defect, contamination, foreign-body inclusion or malicious tampering threatens consumer safety, retrieval, notification, disposal, replacement and restoration and the like the costs directly incurred in a recall are covered by this dedicated recall policy. Where product liability (PL) centres on compensating victims, recall insurance covers the recall costs a business incurs in a voluntary or mandatory recall overall.

    HACCP/GMP-regulated; foreign-body and toxicity risk.

PL vs recall

PL (product liability)Suffered by victims (consumers, third parties) — bodily-injury and property damages
RecallSpent by the business — retrieval, notification, disposal and PR costs

Losses covered — by cost item

Retrieval costCollecting, transporting and storing product
Notification costNotice to consumers and distributors (advertising, press)
DisposalDisposal of recalled product, environmental clean-up
Replacement / repairProviding an equivalent product or repairing
Customer responseCall centre, enquiries, refunds
Emergency responseSpecialist consultants and PR agency
Lost profit (endorsement)Business interruption during the recall
Brand restorationReputation-recovery advertising

Key endorsements

  • Malicious tampering — manipulation of the product by a malicious outsider
  • Product statement error — recall due to advertising or labelling error
  • Overseas-recall extension — local recall of exported product
  • Government-ordered recall response — mandatory recall
  • Lost profit (BI) — lost revenue during the recall

Losses not covered (main exclusions)

  • The policyholder's wilful acts
  • Leaving a known product defect unaddressed
  • Recall arising from IP or patent disputes
  • Third-party damages and fines (the PL area)
  • Delay costs in customs or import/export procedures

Why the surrender value may be less than the premiums paid, or nil

The surrender value is the amount paid if the contract is cancelled early. Unlike bank savings, insurance combines risk protection and savings: part of the premium is paid out as claims to other policyholders who suffer accidents, and part covers the insurer's operating expenses, so the surrender value on early cancellation may be less than the premiums paid, or nil.

When you need recall insurance

Recall-trigger patterns by sector — a five-scenario self-check

🥫

Food and beverage defect recall

Recall from foreign bodies, bacteria or missing allergen labelling. Both an MFDS order and a voluntary recall need trigger review.

🔌

Electrical and electronic defect recall

Safety defects such as battery fire, overheating or electric shock. Includes post-certification management costs for domestic KC and overseas (CE, UL).

🚗

Vehicle and parts recall

Brake, airbag or software defects. Both voluntary corrective action and government-ordered recall are within cover.

🏥

Pharmaceutical and medical-device recall

Side-effects, foreign-body inclusion or labelling error. A mandatory trigger on an MFDS recall order; notification and disposal costs are large.

🌎

Overseas recall after export

Overseas recall orders such as US CPSC and EU RAPEX. A Korea-only territory wording is insufficient — a territory-extension endorsement is essential.

A dispute pattern seen in the field

A food manufacturer's own QC detected a foreign body in part of its product and decided on a voluntary recall. On top of retrieval, customer-notification and disposal costs, external PR crisis-management costs accumulated; but because the wording's definition of the trigger for a voluntary recall was ambiguous, confirming the scope with the insurer took time. Unlike a mandatory recall, the trigger conditions for a voluntary recall differ by insurer, so this case shows that clarifying voluntary-recall cover at the application stage is key.

Three things easily missed when buying recall cover

The wording and structure points decision-makers most often overlook

  • 1

    The definition of “recall costs” differs by wording

    Some wordings cover only retrieval costs, while others include notification, advertising, disposal, PR, consulting and lost operating profit. If the covered scope and limit for each cost item is not checked explicitly at application, the claimable scope can differ when an incident occurs.

  • 2

    The trigger conditions for a voluntary recall

    A government-ordered mandatory recall usually triggers automatically, but a voluntary recall requires meeting wording conditions such as reasonable awareness of a defect and proof of potential harm. If these conditions are ambiguous, confirming cover when an incident occurs can take longer.

  • 3

    Overseas (USA, EU) recall territory limits

    The base wording usually covers only recalls within Korea. If exports are significant or you may be exposed to overseas recall orders such as US CPSC or EU RAPEX, a territory-extension endorsement or a global recall wording is needed separately.

Frequently asked questions

The questions asked most when considering recall insurance

Is recall insurance mandatory?

Recall insurance is usually a voluntary cover. However, it is sometimes required as a de-facto condition at the certification stage — for industrial-convergence new-product conformity, or some medical-device and auto-part certifications — and buyers may require proof of recall cover under export contracts.

What items do recall costs include?

Items usually covered include product-retrieval costs, customer-notification and advertising costs, disposal and reprocessing of recalled product, external consulting and PR crisis-management costs, and part of lost operating profit. Items and limits differ by wording, so checking the cost-definition scope at application is key.

How does cover differ for voluntary vs mandatory recalls?

A government-ordered mandatory recall usually triggers cover automatically, while a voluntary recall must meet the wording's trigger conditions (e.g. reasonable awareness of a defect, proof of potential harm) before cover is considered. The definition of the voluntary-recall trigger varies considerably by insurer.

Are overseas (USA, EU) recalls covered too?

The base wording usually covers only recalls within Korea. For overseas-market recalls such as the USA and EU, a territory-extension endorsement or a global recall wording is needed separately, and the extendable scope and additional premium differ by insurer.

How long from the recall decision to payment?

Because the process includes reviewing whether the trigger is met, submitting documents and verifying cost evidence, it usually takes weeks to months. If immediate funds are needed in a crisis, notifying the insurer in advance and managing cost evidence systematically helps the payment timeline.

How does it differ from PL insurance?

PL's core scope is compensation for third-party harm from a product defect, while recall's core scope is the retrieval, disposal, notification and PR costs of the defective product itself. PL and recall are complementary, and both often respond at once when a defect incident occurs, so they are usually designed together.

How is the premium calculated?

The insurer calculates it based on sector (food, electronics, automotive, medical, etc.), revenue, export share, past recall history and whether a QC system is in place. The exact premium and extendable territory are confirmed after underwriting by insurers such as AIG, Chubb, DB, Hyundai, KB and Meritz.
