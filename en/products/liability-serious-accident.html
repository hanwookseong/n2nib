Overview

Covers the civil and criminal defense costs and victim / bereaved-family compensation that management officers and companies can face under the Serious Accidents Punishment Act.

It responds to the personal and corporate exposure created by serious workplace and public-safety accidents.

Key features

  • 01
    Criminal defense costs

    Legal defense for management under the Act.

  • 02
    Civil compensation

    Damages to victims and bereaved families.

  • 03
    Management & company

    Covers the management officer and the company.

  • 04
    Crisis & investigation

    Investigation and crisis-response support.

Who needs it

  • 01
    Manufacturers & heavy industry

    Higher-hazard operations.

  • 02
    Construction

    Sites exposed to serious-accident risk.

  • 03
    Logistics & facilities

    Operations with public and worker safety exposure.

  • 04
    Management officers

    Representatives accountable under the Act.

Main losses covered

  • Criminal defense costs for management under the Act
  • Civil compensation to victims and bereaved families
  • Investigation, crisis-response and legal costs

Main endorsements

  • 01
    D&O coordination

    Coordinates with directors’ & officers’ cover.

  • 02
    Excess workers’ comp

    Pairs with excess employer-liability cover.

  • 03
    Public liability

    Third-party public-safety exposures.

Main exclusions

  • Fines and penalties imposed as punishment
  • Deliberate, reckless or wilful violation of safety duties
  • Liability known or foreseeable before inception
  • Amounts payable as statutory benefits
  • Matters outside the scope of the Act, by wording

Conditions & process

  • Underwriting reflects industry, headcount and safety management
  • Cover is coordinated with D&O and employer-liability programmes
  • Defense-cost and compensation limits are set at inception

What we need to quote

  • 01
    Industry & scale

    Industry, headcount and site profile.

  • 02
    Safety management

    Safety and health management system.

  • 03
    Existing cover

    D&O and employer-liability arrangements.

  • 04
    History & limit

    Incident history and the limit required.

Other notes

  • Criminal fines imposed as punishment are not insurable
  • Final terms and premium are confirmed after insurer underwriting
