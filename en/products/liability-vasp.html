Overview

Under Article 7 of the Specific Financial Information Act, this is an essential product for exchanges, custody businesses and wallet operators reported and accepted as VASPs — to indemnify user losses from hacking, system failure or insider fraud, or to substitute the statutory reserve obligation with insurance.

Statutory reserve / insurance standard

  • A reserve of at least 5% of average daily trading value, or insurance
  • Minimum KRW 3.0 billion; the maximum varies by exchange
  • Mandatory for KRW-market exchanges; partially applied to coin-market exchanges

Legal basis

  • Act on Reporting and Use of Specific Financial Transaction Information (Specific Financial Information Act), Article 7
  • Virtual Asset User Protection Act (effective 19 Jul 2024)
  • FSC / FSS guidelines

Main losses covered

  • External hacking — private-key theft, smart-contract exploits
  • System failure — transaction errors from a system outage
  • Insider fraud — asset theft by employees or officers
  • Hot-wallet attacks · DDoS / APT response
  • Customer personal-data breach
  • Restoration, legal, consulting, defense and forensic-investigation costs

Key endorsements

  • Cold Storage extension — offline-wallet hacking
  • Smart Contract Audit Fail — loss from contract vulnerabilities
  • Key Management Failure — key-management system failure
  • Regulatory Fines response — FSS / FIU penalty response
  • Cyber Extortion — ransomware negotiation

Main exclusions

  • Willful acts and fraud
  • Price movement / FX loss (decline in asset value)
  • The operator's own KRW / cash losses
  • Incidents during regulatory-breach operation
  • Market manipulation / wash trading
  • Nuclear, war and acts of state

Conditions & process

  • Policy period 1 year (Claims-Made basis)
  • Insurer Hyundai Marine & Fire (limited domestic capacity · placed with overseas reinsurers)
  • Limit at least the statutory minimum KRW 3.0 billion (set by trading scale)
  • Turnaround 2–4 weeks for standard custody firms / 1–2 months for exchanges & global
  • Setting a Retroactive Date is decisive

What we need to quote

  • Business-registration certificate · corporate register
  • VASP report-acceptance certificate
  • ISMS certificate
  • Average daily trading value · custody AUM
  • 6-month monthly transaction summary
  • Internal-control system (ISMS-P)
  • Cold / Hot wallet ratio
  • Past hacking / outage history
  • Smart Contract Audit report
Underwriting key Cold Storage of 95%+ improves the rate. Claims-Made + Retroactive Date is decisive. Cooperation with overseas reinsurers is essential.

Nullity of the contract

If the insured event has already occurred at the time the contract is made, the contract is void. However, where the contract is void due to the company's intent or negligence, or where the company knew or could have known of the nullity before acceptance yet did not refund the premium, the company refunds the premium with interest calculated at the policy-loan rate published by the Korea Insurance Development Institute, compounded annually, for the period from the day after payment to the day of refund.

A dispute pattern seen in the field

A VASP's risk does not lie in external hacking alone. External attacks such as private-key theft or smart-contract exploits, transaction errors from system failure, and insider fraud such as asset theft by employees — there are several paths to loss of user assets. VASP liability insurance covers the indemnity of user losses from such incidents, together with restoration, forensic and legal costs. But there is a clear boundary. Decline in asset value from virtual-asset price movement or FX, the operator's own KRW/cash losses, incidents during non-compliant operation, and market manipulation or wash trading are excluded. This insurance addresses ‘safekeeping and operational incidents’, not ‘investment loss’.

3 things easily missed when buying VASP liability insurance

The wording and structure items decision-makers most often overlook

  • 1

    Price movement & investment loss are not covered

    Loss of asset value from a fall in virtual-asset prices or FX is excluded. This insurance covers safekeeping and operational incidents such as hacking, system failure and insider fraud.

  • 2

    The relationship between the statutory reserve and insurance

    The Specific Financial Information Act and the Virtual Asset User Protection Act require a reserve or insurance of a set standard to cover user losses. Confirm precisely how the policy satisfies the legal requirement.

  • 3

    Incidents during non-compliant operation are excluded

    Incidents during operation that fails to meet reporting/certification requirements are excluded. Separately from insurance, you must meet the requirements of the Specific Financial Information Act and related guidelines.

Frequently asked questions

The questions asked most when considering VASP liability insurance

Is VASP liability insurance compulsory?

The Specific Financial Information Act and the Virtual Asset User Protection Act require virtual-asset service providers to hold a reserve or insurance of a set standard to cover user losses from hacking, system failure and similar incidents. The applicable threshold differs by business type and scale, so confirm it against the relevant law and guidelines.

What incidents does it cover?

It covers user losses from external hacking (private-key theft, smart-contract exploits), transaction errors from system failure, insider fraud such as asset theft by employees, hot-wallet attacks and customer data breaches, together with restoration, forensic and legal costs.

Are losses from a fall in virtual-asset prices covered?

No. Decline in asset value from virtual-asset price movement or FX is excluded. This insurance covers safekeeping and operational incidents such as hacking, system failure and insider fraud, and does not address investment loss.

Can insurance substitute the statutory reserve obligation?

The Specific Financial Information Act and the Virtual Asset User Protection Act require a reserve or insurance of a set standard to cover user losses. Whether insurance can satisfy part or all of the legal requirement depends on business type and the regulatory standard, so confirm it precisely.

When is cover restricted?

Willful acts and fraud, price/FX loss, the operator's own KRW/cash losses, incidents during regulatory-breach operation, and market manipulation or wash trading are excluded.

How is the premium calculated?

The insurer calculates it based on business type (exchange/custody/wallet), average daily trading value and custody AUM, security level such as the cold/hot wallet ratio, the limit, and past hacking/outage history. The exact premium and terms are confirmed after underwriting by insurers such as AIG, Chubb, DB, Hyundai, KB and Meritz.
