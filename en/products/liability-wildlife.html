Why the surrender value may be less than the premiums paid, or nil

The surrender value is the amount paid if the contract is cancelled early. Unlike bank savings, insurance combines risk protection and savings: part of the premium is paid out as claims to other policyholders who suffer accidents, and part covers the insurer's operating expenses, so the surrender value on early cancellation may be less than the premiums paid, or nil.

Depositor protection

  • (However, a policy whose policyholder and premium payer is a corporation is not protected.)

A dispute pattern seen in the field

Wildlife damage compensation insurance has a different take-out structure from other liability insurance. The party that takes out cover is not an individual operator but an administrative body such as a metropolitan or basic local government, and the insurance serves to secure the funds the local government uses to compensate residents and farms for wildlife harm. It covers the compensation and the administrative cost of compensation for crop damage by wildlife such as wild boar and water deer, livestock and bee-colony deaths, traffic accidents and personal harm from animals running onto a road, and vehicle/facility damage. But where a person deliberately lured the wildlife, an accident during hunting or capture, or harm by an animal over which ownership is claimed or a trained animal, are within the exclusions.

Frequently asked questions

The questions decision-makers ask most when considering wildlife damage compensation insurance

What is wildlife damage compensation insurance?

It is insurance to secure the funds a local government uses to compensate residents and farms for harm caused by wildlife. It covers the compensation and the administrative cost of compensation for crop damage, livestock and bee-colony deaths, personal accidents including traffic accidents, and vehicle/facility damage.

Who takes it out?

Not individual operators but administrative bodies — metropolitan and basic local governments, the Korea Forest Service and its agencies, and natural-park management bodies.

Are traffic accidents covered?

Vehicle accidents caused by wildlife running onto a road, and the resulting personal and vehicle harm, are within the cover. The specific scope should be checked in the wording and the compensation guidelines.

On what basis is compensation made?

Compensation follows the standard compensation guidelines. Check that the limit is designed to match the scale of harm in the area of jurisdiction and the guidelines.

In what cases is compensation limited?

Exclusions include a person deliberately luring wildlife, accidents during hunting or capture, and harm by an animal over which ownership is claimed or by a trained animal.

How is the premium assessed?

The insurer assesses it on the area and population of the jurisdiction, past wildlife-damage statistics, the wildlife-habitat status and the limit. The exact premium and acceptance terms are confirmed after underwriting by insurers such as AIG, Chubb, DB, KB, Meritz and Hyundai.
