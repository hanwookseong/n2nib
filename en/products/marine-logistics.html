Overview

Logistics Comprehensive insurance covers the whole-business risk of a logistics company (3PL, forwarder, warehouse operator, carrier) under a single policy.

Cargo liability, warehouse-keeper's liability, property, machinery, general liability, cyber and workers' compensation can all be designed in one place — suited to mid-size logistics companies that need unified contract management and claims response.

Component modules

  • A
    Cargo liability

    Legal liability of forwarders, carriers and warehouse operators.

  • B
    Property (warehouse / office)

    Distribution-center and terminal assets.

  • C
    Machinery (MB)

    Conveyors, cranes, forklifts, refrigeration.

  • D
    General Liability (GL)

    Third-party accidents on the premises.

  • E
    Workers' compensation

    Logistics-worker accidents.

  • F
    Cyber / E&O

    Logistics-system failure / data-error liability.

  • G
    Carrier's vehicle endorsement

    Loss to cargo on the company's own vehicles.

  • 02

  • 03

Main losses covered (by module)

  • Cargo — legal liability for loss in transit or storage
  • Warehouse / terminal — fire, wind/flood, theft, damage
  • Machinery (MB) — damage to conveyors, cranes, refrigeration
  • General Liability (GL) — third-party accidents on the premises
  • Workers' comp — logistics-worker accident benefits
  • Cyber / E&O — WMS / TMS failure or data-error liability
  • Carrier's cargo — loss to cargo on the company's own vehicles

Main endorsements

  • Cold Chain — temperature control for refrigerated / frozen logistics
  • NVOCC — own-B/L issuance liability
  • Cross-border — during customs clearance
  • Event logistics — exhibitions / fairs
  • Last-mile delivery — two-wheelers / quick service
  • Customs Bond — bonded warehouse

Main exclusions

  • Wilful misconduct / gross negligence
  • Pure economic loss from transit / storage delay
  • Liability aggravated beyond agreement (without endorsement)
  • Insufficient packing
  • Nuclear / war
  • Breach of dangerous-goods handling duties in the warehouse
