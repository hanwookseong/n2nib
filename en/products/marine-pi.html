Overview

While Hull & Machinery covers physical loss to the hull and part of the collision liability, Protection & Indemnity (P&I) comprehensively covers the personal and legal liabilities a shipowner bears beyond that.

It can be used through the commercial P&I market, separately from or alongside an international P&I Club.

Who needs it

  • 01
    Shipping / passenger lines

    Container ships, passenger ships, ferries.

  • 02
    Fishing owners

    Ocean and coastal fishing vessels.

  • 03
    Marine civil works / towage

    Barges, tugs, work boats.

  • 04
    Small-vessel operators

    Coastal and fishing boats.

Basis / standards

Commercial Act (maritime), Oil Pollution Compensation Act (CLC / OPA 90), Seafarers Act.

Main losses covered

  • Crew injury / death, repatriation and treatment
  • Oil-pollution loss and clean-up costs
  • Cargo loss / damage liability
  • The 1/4 of collision liability (complementing Hull)
  • Passenger injury / death liability
  • Wreck removal
  • Salvage
  • Fines / penalties (some endorsements)

Main endorsements

  • Charterer's P&I — for charterers
  • Small-vessel package — coastal package
  • CLC / OPA 90 compliance — international-convention requirements

Main exclusions

  • Wilful misconduct; unseaworthiness (where known)
  • War / piracy (separate War endorsement)
  • Crew desertion or criminal acts
  • Government seizure / detention
  • Damage to the vessel itself (covered by Hull)
