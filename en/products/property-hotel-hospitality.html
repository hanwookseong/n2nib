Overview

A package that manages the varied risks of accommodation and tourism businesses under one policy — fire, theft, guest accidents, food poisoning and business interruption across rooms, lobby, banquet halls, F&B, pools, spas and golf facilities.

Key features

  • 01
    Single-policy package

    Many facility exposures combined in one document.

  • 02
    Business interruption

    Lost revenue during a covered shutdown.

  • 03
    Guest-accident cover

    Falls, slips and pool incidents.

  • 04
    Compulsory liability tie-in

    Can combine compulsory multi-use fire liability.

Who needs it

  • 01
    Hotels & resorts

    Full-service accommodation.

  • 02
    Condos & pensions

    Condominiums, pensions and guesthouses.

  • 03
    Motels

    Roadside and urban motels.

  • 04
    Mixed F&B venues

    Properties with restaurants, banquets and pools.

Main losses covered

  • Fire, explosion, storm/flood and earthquake (by endorsement)
  • Business-interruption loss and revenue decline
  • Guest bodily injury (falls, slips, drowning)
  • F&B food poisoning and foreign-object incidents
  • Theft, robbery and pickpocketing
  • Innkeeper's liability for guests' valuables
  • Legal defense costs

Main exclusions

  • Willful acts and gross negligence
  • Gradual deterioration and mould
  • War, terrorism and nuclear risks
  • Self-harm by intoxicated guests
  • Operation without the required licence

Conditions & process

  • Modules are configured by facility mix — rooms, F&B, pool and banquet
  • Compulsory multi-use fire liability can be combined
  • Final terms, limits and premium are confirmed after insurer underwriting

What we need to quote

  • 01
    Licences

    Tourism / accommodation registration.

  • 02
    Building details

    Building ledger, floor plan and room count.

  • 03
    Facility mix

    F&B, banquet and pool operations.

  • 04
    Financials & claims

    12–24 months of revenue and 3-year loss history.

Other notes

  • Storm/flood and earthquake are added by endorsement
  • Final terms, limits and premium are confirmed after insurer underwriting
