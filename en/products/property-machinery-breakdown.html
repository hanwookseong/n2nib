Overview

Machinery breakdown insurance covers sudden and accidental physical damage to machinery from internal causes — mechanical or electrical breakdown, design or material defect, short circuit, overload and operator error.

It complements fire insurance, which excludes internal damage to machinery.

Key features

  • 01
    Internal breakdown cover

    Answers the internal machinery damage that fire policies exclude.

  • 02
    Repair / replacement

    Repair or replacement of the damaged parts and components.

  • 03
    Operator error & electrical fault

    Damage from operator mistakes, short circuits and electrical faults.

  • 04
    Pairs with MLoP

    Machinery loss-of-profit can be added to cover resulting downtime.

Who needs it

  • 01
    Manufacturers

    Plants relying on production machinery.

  • 02
    Power & utilities

    Generation and utility plant.

  • 03
    Heavy equipment

    Operators of heavy or specialised machinery.

  • 04
    Buildings with critical M&E

    Chillers, transformers, lifts and pumps.

Main losses covered

  • Repair or replacement of machinery damaged by sudden mechanical / electrical breakdown
  • On-site dismantling, re-erection and clean-up costs
  • Damage from electrical faults, short circuits and operator error

Main endorsements

  • 01
    Machinery loss of profit (MLoP)

    Business interruption arising from the machinery breakdown.

  • 02
    Express / air freight

    Expediting costs to speed up repair or replacement.

  • 03
    Surrounding property

    Damage to property around the machinery caused by the breakdown.

Main exclusions

  • Wear and tear and gradual deterioration
  • Pre-existing defects known before inception
  • Fire and external perils (covered under property insurance)
  • Consumables — belts, tools, dies, refractories
  • Purely cosmetic damage not affecting function

Conditions & process

  • Underwriting is based on a schedule of machinery with values
  • Age and maintenance regime affect terms
  • MLoP requires a gross-profit and indemnity-period basis

What we need to quote

  • 01
    Machinery schedule

    Type, value and year of the machinery to be insured.

  • 02
    Maintenance

    Maintenance and inspection regime.

  • 03
    Loss history

    Past breakdowns or claims.

  • 04
    MLoP & limit

    Loss-of-profit needs and the limit required.

Other notes

  • New-replacement-value vs market-value basis affects settlement
  • Final terms and premium are confirmed after insurer underwriting
