Overview

A two-section programme for power plants, factories and process plants. Section I covers sudden and accidental physical damage to machinery and electrical equipment; Section II covers the machinery loss of profit / business interruption arising from that damage.

Key features

  • 01
    Section I + Section II

    Material damage and the resulting business interruption in one programme.

  • 02
    Wide causes

    Internal breakdown and a broad range of sudden, accidental causes.

  • 03
    For power / process plants

    Designed for generation, process and heavy industry.

  • 04
    Integrated machinery risk

    One programme for damage and downtime.

Who needs it

  • 01
    Power generation

    Thermal, combined-cycle and renewable plant.

  • 02
    Heavy / process manufacturing

    Continuous-process and heavy industry.

  • 03
    Petrochemical / plant

    Refineries, chemical and process plants.

  • 04
    Utilities & infrastructure

    Critical utility and infrastructure operators.

Main losses covered

  • Section I — sudden & accidental physical damage to machinery and electrical equipment
  • Section II — loss of gross profit during the resulting interruption
  • Dismantling, re-erection and expediting costs

Main endorsements

  • 01
    Extended indemnity period

    Longer recovery period for major machinery losses.

  • 02
    Expediting costs

    Additional costs to accelerate repair and restart.

  • 03
    Surrounding property

    Property around the machinery damaged by the event.

Main exclusions

  • Wear and tear and gradual deterioration
  • Known defects disclosed or knowable before inception
  • Uninsured perils and consequential loss outside Section II
  • Consumables and routine maintenance items
  • War, nuclear and cyber perils

Conditions & process

  • A machinery schedule, values and a BI worksheet are required
  • An engineering survey may be required for larger plant
  • Section II follows the gross-profit and indemnity-period basis

What we need to quote

  • 01
    Machinery schedule

    Plant and electrical equipment with values.

  • 02
    Gross profit

    Gross profit and the indemnity period for Section II.

  • 03
    Survey data

    Maintenance, inspection and any engineering survey.

  • 04
    Loss history & limits

    Past losses and the limits required.

Other notes

  • Deductibles often scale with machinery value
  • Final terms and premium are confirmed after insurer underwriting
