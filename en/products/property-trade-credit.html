Trade Credit Insurance

From a licensed insurance broker

N2N Insurance Brokerage independently represents the client as an FSS-registered broker (Reg. No. 2026-012201). For trade credit we place and compare cover through Chubb and specialist trade-credit channels (including global credit insurers and K-SURE) to suit your domestic and export receivables risk. Each insurer’s full wording is provided at application and binding.

Overview

Trade credit insurance covers the risk of non-payment of trade receivables arising from a buyer’s insolvency, legal restructuring or protracted default, for both domestic and export sales.

Cover is placed through Chubb and specialist trade-credit channels, with integrated buyer-credit assessment and receivables management.

Key features

  • 01
    Insolvency & default

    Indemnity for buyer insolvency, restructuring and protracted default.

  • 02
    Domestic + export

    Domestic and export receivables under one programme.

  • 03
    Buyer credit limits

    Buyer assessment and credit limits set per customer.

  • 04
    Receivables management

    Monitoring and collections support reduce bad-debt risk.

Who needs it

  • 01
    Exporters & importers

    Companies trading internationally on open account.

  • 02
    Manufacturers / distributors

    Sellers extending credit terms to buyers.

  • 03
    Trading companies

    Businesses with high receivables turnover.

  • 04
    Concentrated buyer risk

    Where a few large buyers dominate sales.

Main losses covered

  • Unpaid receivables from buyer insolvency or legal restructuring
  • Protracted default — non-payment after an agreed waiting period
  • Export sales, with political-risk cover by endorsement

Main endorsements

  • 01
    Political risk

    Non-payment caused by political events on export sales.

  • 02
    Single-buyer / whole-turnover

    Cover for one key buyer or the whole sales ledger.

  • 03
    Pre-credit risk

    Risk arising before goods are delivered, subject to terms.

Main exclusions

  • Disputed debts until the dispute is resolved
  • Receivables already overdue at inception
  • Sales outside the agreed payment terms or credit limit
  • Non-delivery and performance disputes
  • Fines, penalties and interest on late payment

Conditions & process

  • Whole-turnover or single-buyer structure is agreed at the outset
  • Buyer credit limits are assessed and monitored by the insurer
  • Claims follow the waiting period and collection process

What we need to quote

  • 01
    Turnover

    Annual insurable turnover, domestic and export.

  • 02
    Buyer portfolio

    Key buyers, countries and requested credit limits.

  • 03
    Terms

    Standard payment terms and credit-control process.

  • 04
    Bad-debt history

    Past defaults and the structure required.

Other notes

  • Cover is usually subject to an insured percentage and limits per buyer
  • Final terms and premium are confirmed after insurer underwriting
