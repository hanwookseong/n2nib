Overview

Theft insurance covers the value and incidental loss when goods, stock, cash and valuables kept at a premises, store, warehouse or office are lost through robbery, theft, intrusion or night break-in.

Its scope is broader than the theft endorsement on fire insurance, and cover can be designed in detail down to cash in store, safe and transit.

Key features

  • 01
    Goods + cash + valuables

    Stock, money, securities and precious metals set out in detail.

  • 02
    Premises-damage extension

    Repair cost for doors / windows / safe damaged in the theft.

  • 03
    Cash-in-transit endorsement

    Theft of cash in transit to / from the bank.

  • 04
    CCTV / security discount

    Rate discount when linked to security systems / providers.

Who needs it

  • 01
    Jewellery / luxury / electronics shops

    Retailers holding high-cash-value stock.

  • 02
    Warehouses / distribution centres

    Holding large stock overnight / on holidays.

  • 03
    Clinics / pharmacies / convenience stores

    Holding cash, medicines, electronics.

Main losses covered

  • Theft of goods, stock, cash, valuables by robbery / theft / night intrusion
  • Repair cost for premises damage (doors, windows, safe) during the theft
  • On-premises robbery by force / threat
  • Theft of cash in transit to / from the bank (endorsement)

Main endorsements

  • Cash / securities in transit
  • Contents in the safe
  • Customers' bailed goods
  • Cash in vending machines / ATMs

Main exclusions

  • Wilful act or collusion of the insured, employees or cohabitants
  • Simple loss / carelessness with no sign of intrusion
  • Theft while doors / windows / safe were left unlocked
  • Vague shortfall found only on stock-take
  • War, civil commotion, terrorism, nuclear risk

What we need to quote

  • Premises address, opening hours, area
  • Average stock value, maximum held value, cash limit
  • Locks, safe, CCTV, security-provider contract
  • Theft history for the last 3–5 years

Other notes

  • On a loss, always report to the police and submit a loss statement.
  • Stock-take ledgers and CCTV records are key evidence.
  • Adjust the sum insured when the value of valuables held changes.

Cooling-off / duty of disclosure

  • Cooling-off (cancellation) within 15 days of receiving the certificate (excluding professional financial consumers).
  • Breach of the duty to explain material matters or a missing signature may allow cancellation within 3 months.
  • Breach of the duty of disclosure may lead to cancellation or reduced indemnity.
