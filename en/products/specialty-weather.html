Parametric Weather Insurance

⚖
From a licensed insurance broker

N2N Insurance Brokerage independently represents the client as an FSS-registered broker (Reg. No. 2026-012201). Parametric weather cover is structured with specialist parametric and index capacity — we design the trigger, index source and payout to match your weather exposure. Each insurer’s full wording is provided at application and binding.

Overview

Parametric weather insurance pays a pre-agreed amount when an objective weather index — rainfall, snowfall, temperature or wind speed — breaches the contract trigger. Settlement is fast and transparent, without traditional loss adjustment.

It is used by leisure, tourism, agriculture and construction businesses whose revenue depends on the weather.

Key features

  • 01
    Index-based payout

    Payout is driven by an objective weather index, not a loss assessment.

  • 02
    No loss adjustment

    No claims investigation — the trigger settles the claim.

  • 03
    Custom trigger

    Trigger, index source and payout are designed to your exposure.

  • 04
    Fast settlement

    Payout follows quickly once the index is confirmed.

Who needs it

  • 01
    Leisure & tourism

    Operators whose revenue depends on the weather.

  • 02
    Outdoor events

    Festivals and events exposed to rain or wind.

  • 03
    Agriculture

    Farms and greenhouses exposed to drought or frost.

  • 04
    Construction & energy

    Projects sensitive to rainfall, temperature or wind.

Main losses covered

  • A pre-agreed payout when the weather index breaches the trigger
  • Excess or deficit of rainfall, snowfall, temperature or wind
  • Revenue-linked payout structures, by design

Main endorsements

  • 01
    Multi-peril index

    Combines more than one weather variable in the trigger.

  • 02
    Seasonal cover

    Cover aligned to a defined season or window.

  • 03
    Revenue-linked payout

    Payout scaled to the insured’s revenue exposure.

Main exclusions

  • Events outside the defined index or cover period
  • Failure or revision of the data source, subject to wording
  • Losses from causes other than the insured weather index
  • Basis risk — mismatch between the index and the actual loss
  • Consequential and uninsured perils

Conditions & process

  • The peril, index source, trigger and payout are defined at inception
  • An independent index / data provider settles the trigger
  • Basis risk is reviewed when designing the structure

What we need to quote

  • 01
    Peril & location

    The weather peril and the site or region.

  • 02
    Index source

    The preferred weather station or data source.

  • 03
    Trigger levels

    The threshold(s) that should trigger payout.

  • 04
    Period & payout

    The cover period and the payout required.

Other notes

  • Parametric payout may differ from the actual loss (basis risk)
  • Final terms and premium are confirmed after insurer underwriting
