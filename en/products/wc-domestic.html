Overview

Domestic Workers' Compensation (Employer's Liability) covers the employer's civil liability that exceeds the statutory industrial-accident (WC) benefit when a worker is injured at work.

State industrial-accident insurance pays first; the excess that the employer (including main contractor and subcontractor) must bear under civil law is covered here. It is effectively essential in construction, shipbuilding and manufacturing prime / sub-contracting, where the certificate is required as a contract attachment.

Key features

  • 01
    Excess over industrial-accident benefit

    Covers the employer's civil liability (consolation money, lost earnings) beyond the statutory benefit.

  • 02
    Annual or per-project

    Annual site-wide cover or a per-project (Project Basis) policy.

  • 03
    Includes subcontractor workers

    Insured scope can extend to subcontractor workers to match the main contractor's liability.

  • 04
    Serious-Accidents Act link

    Designed alongside management liability under the Serious Accidents Punishment Act.

Who needs it

  • 01
    Construction prime / subcontractors

    Certificate required on award — public and private works.

  • 02
    Manufacturing / shipbuilding / heavy industry

    Sectors with high accident frequency / severity.

  • 03
    Logistics / transport / energy

    Distribution centres, power plants, fuel stations.

Main losses covered

  • Civil damages exceeding the industrial-accident benefit for death, permanent disability and treatment
  • Consolation money, lost earnings and funeral costs for the injured worker and bereaved family
  • Litigation and attorney costs borne by the employer
  • First-aid and emergency-prevention costs
  • Handling of indemnity disputes between main contractor and subcontractor

Main endorsements

  • Employer's Liability
  • Subcontractor-worker extension
  • Commuting-accident extension
  • Dispatched / agency-worker cover

Main exclusions

  • Employer's wilful unlawful act
  • The portion fully covered by industrial-accident insurance (no double recovery)
  • Liability aggravated by contract
  • Accidents while driving without a licence or under the influence
  • Accidents during private activity unrelated to work
  • War / nuclear-related accidents

What we need to quote

  • Industry, main work types, number of regular workers
  • Annual revenue or contract value
  • Industrial-accident / fatality history for the last 3–5 years
  • Desired limits and deductibles
  • Main contractor / principal requirements (limits, additional insureds)

Other notes

  • Statutory industrial-accident insurance is a separate legal duty; this policy covers the civil liability beyond it.
  • Per-project policies are adjusted when the contract value changes.
  • Notify the insurer immediately on a fatality / serious injury (within 24 hours recommended).

Cooling-off / duty of disclosure

  • Cooling-off (cancellation) within 15 days of receiving the certificate (excluding professional financial consumers).
  • Breach of the duty to explain material matters or a missing signature may allow cancellation within 3 months.
  • Breach of the duty of disclosure may lead to cancellation or reduced indemnity.
