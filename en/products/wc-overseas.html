Overview

Overseas Workers' Compensation (Foreign Voluntary Workers Compensation / Employer's Liability) covers the employer's civil liability for work-related injury to overseas, expatriate and locally hired staff in areas not reached by Korean industrial-accident insurance.

It is designed to international standards aligned with each country's statutory local WC and US / European Employer's Liability forms, and local linked policies can be issued through major global insurers.

Key features

  • 01
    24-hour cover

    Off-duty accidents during the posting are covered (per endorsement).

  • 02
    Country-specific compliance

    Global programme reflecting statutory local-WC requirements by host country (US, China, SE Asia, Middle East).

  • 03
    Repatriation cover

    Repatriation, treatment and funeral costs and emergency medical evacuation (SOS) on a serious accident.

  • 04
    Terrorism / political-risk option

    Terrorism / war / unrest injury endorsements for high-risk postings.

Who needs it

  • 01
    Overseas-project contractors / engineering

    Plant and construction work in the Middle East, SE Asia, Africa.

  • 02
    Multinational manufacturers / traders

    Firms posting and managing expatriates, travellers and local-entity staff.

  • 03
    NGOs / international bodies / public agencies

    ODA, overseas education / medical missions.

Main losses covered

  • Bodily injury, death, treatment and consolation money for workers from overseas work accidents
  • Statutory local WC benefits
  • Employer's excess liability under Korean civil law / Labour Standards Act
  • Repatriation and emergency medical evacuation (SOS) costs
  • Legal damages to bereaved families and injured workers

Main endorsements

  • 24-hour (non-occupational) extension
  • Terrorism / war / SRCC extension
  • Medical evacuation
  • Kidnap & Ransom link

Main exclusions

  • Employer's wilful unlawful act
  • Pre-existing / previously known illness
  • Accidents during illegal stay or visa-less work
  • Weapons of mass destruction / nuclear / radiation
  • Accidents under the influence of alcohol / drugs (by country)
  • Business in designated sanctioned countries (OFAC, etc.)
